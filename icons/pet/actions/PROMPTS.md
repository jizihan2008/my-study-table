# 时芽动作素材

使用内置 image_gen，以 ../shiya.png 为角色参考生成，transparent_background=true。六个文件各有四帧，2×2 排列，按左上、右上、左下、右下读取。

公共提示词：

Create a production animation SPRITE SHEET of the exact chibi character in the reference. One transparent square PNG, EXACTLY FOUR frames in a precise 2-column by 2-row grid of equal square cells. Reading order top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right. NO borders, labels, text or separators. Every cell contains the same character once, in the same style and scale. Full character INCLUDING FEET AND HAIR fully inside its cell with 12 percent transparent padding. Same camera, same face, costume and proportions, aligned root at horizontal cell center and feet baseline at 88 percent cell height; do not scale between frames. Maintain silver-lavender twin ponytails, mint leaf hair ribbons, blue eyes, cobalt/ivory sailor jacket, blue skirt, opaque dark leggings, sneakers, small book pouch. Simplify tiny details for a 170px desktop sprite. Crisp consistent anime cel shading, 2.5 head chibi proportions. Frames must be DIFFERENT poses forming a sequential action, not a concept art collage. Transparent background with real alpha, no floor shadows.
